What to include for an accurate quote

  • Shape + dimensions — round: diameter; square: side; rectangle: length × width (inches or mm, both fine).
  • Thickness — 1/4", 3/8", 1/2", 3/4", or 1" (6–25 mm); tell us the part weight and hand vs. machine use if you're unsure.
  • Glass type + grit — frosted (320–1000) for grinding stages, clear for polishing.
  • Quantity — and whether you want the grit ladder (e.g. 320 + 600 + 1000 + clear).
  • Paste — grit and tin size, if you want supply included.
  • Application + deadline — what you're lapping and when you need it; a photo of the part helps.
  • Shipping address (zip is enough) — for freight estimate on large or thick laps.

Requests are answered by email. We don't collect payment on the site — quotes are confirmed by reply, then invoiced.
